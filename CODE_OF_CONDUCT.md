# Questlock Code of Conduct

Questlock welcomes contributors and users of different backgrounds, identities
and experience levels. Help make its issues, discussions, pull requests and
other project spaces useful and respectful.

## Expected conduct

- Be considerate, constructive and patient. Discuss the work and its evidence.
- Respect differences of opinion, experience and personal circumstances.
- Accept correction, give credit and explain technical disagreement clearly.
- Protect other people's private information and respect security-reporting rules.

Harassment, discriminatory or demeaning remarks, threats, sexual harassment,
deliberate intimidation and publishing someone else's private information
without permission are not acceptable. Do not use project spaces to target
people or continue unwelcome contact.

## Scope

This policy covers project-managed spaces and conduct while representing
Questlock. It applies equally to maintainers, contributors and users.

## Reporting and response

The maintainer listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) handles reports. Use a
private contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile when one is available.
Otherwise, open an issue requesting a private contact route, without naming
the people involved or publishing the report's details. Security
vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports should describe the conduct, relevant context and any immediate
concerns. The maintainer will handle the information with care, limit sharing
to what is needed to investigate and explain any resulting action to affected
people when appropriate. The project has no guaranteed response-time SLA.

Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include clarification, a
warning, removal of content, restrictions on participation or removal from
project spaces. Retaliation for a good-faith report is not acceptable. When a
report concerns the only maintainer, the reporter may request a separate
trusted reviewer as described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
